The system, drawn twice

Your whole interview lives on two maps. The first follows one request from a browser to the database and back. The second follows the system itself: how it is built, watched, and changed. Learn to point at any box and say what it does — and what happens when it dies.

THE ASK

What the 25 minutes covers

Eight domains, 134 questions in the bank. The interviewer picks 1–3 per domain — then asks why until your understanding stops. That follow-up is the interview.

How to prep, in one line: explain each box out loud, in your own words, from your own build. If you use a word you can't define, stop — "sort of" counts as "no". Name the mechanism and you pass even if you forget the name; name the thing without the mechanism and you don't.
